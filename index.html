Sync & backup

1. Keep your ticks in your Google Drive (automatic)

Paste the Web app URL and the secret from your Google Apps Script. They are kept in this browser only - they are not part of the page file. Your Google Sheet gets a Log tab (one new row for every tick) and a Daily tab (one row per day).

The setup link contains your URL and secret. Open it on your phone or another computer to connect there without typing, and don't post it anywhere public.

2. Backup file

Download your ticks as a small file you can keep anywhere (for example in Drive), and load it back on any device.

Ticks and your day-wise history always save on this device. Connect Google Drive under Sync & backup to keep them across devices and get a Log and Daily tab in your Sheet. Class sheets and Module 8 come from your channel paste, not from your original tracker PDF.
